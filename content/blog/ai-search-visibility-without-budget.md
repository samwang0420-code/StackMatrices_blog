# How to Dominate AI Search Visibility Without Breaking Your Marketing Budget

## The Visibility Trap

Back in the old SEO days, success was simple: you checked your keyword rankings, and you knew where you stood. Those days are over.

Now we're in the GEO era—Generative Engine Optimization. Instead of just ranking on Google, you need to show up when people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude for recommendations.

Here's the problem: everyone wants to "track everything." Every platform, every prompt, every mention. And here's what happens—costs spiral out of control, and you're left with a mess of data that tells you nothing useful.

**The truth:** You don't need to track every mention. You need to track the *right* mentions.

## Step 1: Find Where Your Patients Actually Are

Don't guess which AI platform your patients use. Look at your data.

In GA4, go to **Reports → Acquisition → Traffic Acquisition**. Change the dimension to "Session source/medium." Look for referrers like "AI Tools" or "ChatGPT."

A few things to keep in mind:

- **Demographics matter.** Studies show women are more likely to use ChatGPT than some alternatives. Millennials lead in using AI for purchasing decisions.
- **Geography matters.** UK users tend to use AI more daily than US users. Check your regional data.
- **Content wins.** See which pages get AI traffic? That's your winning content. Double down on it.

## Step 2: Check How AI Actually Sees You

Before you build any prompt list, understand what the AI models actually know about your practice.

Here's a quick way to check:

1. Use **Moz Domain Overview** to see what topics Google associates with your site
2. Search your brand on Google and check the "People Also Search For" panel—this shows what entities Google links to you
3. **Reality check:** You might think your competitors are X, Y, and Z. But the AI models might see you differently. Align your tracking with how the models actually perceive you, not how you *think* they should.

## Step 3: Stop Obsessing Over Branded Searches

Here's a mistake we see constantly: practices get excited when an AI mentions their brand name.

But here's the thing—if you ask an AI "Does Dr. Smith's clinic have good reviews?", of course it'll mention Dr. Smith. That's not visibility. That's just answering the question.

Real visibility shows up in **neutral searches**—when someone asks "Who does the best Botox in Los Angeles?" and your practice gets mentioned without you even being named.

What to actually track:

- **Comparison searches** ("Botox vs fillers—which is better for me?")
- **Recommendation searches** ("What's the best med spa near me?")
- **Question searches** ("How long does Botox last?")

These are the conversations that bring new patients through your door.

## The Bottom Line

You don't need a massive tracking budget. You need:

1. **Know your audience** — Check GA4 to see which AI platforms they actually use
2. **Know your entity** — Understand how AI models perceive your practice vs. competitors
3. **Track the right things** — Focus on neutral, high-intent searches, not just brand mentions

That's how you dominate AI search without blowing your budget.
